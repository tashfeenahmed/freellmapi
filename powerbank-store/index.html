FAQ

Can I take a power bank on a plane?

Yes — power banks under 100 Wh (about 27,000 mAh) are allowed in carry-on luggage on most airlines.

How do I pay with Apple Pay?

Click checkout and choose Apple Pay on Safari / iPhone / Mac. Visa and other cards work on every device.

Will it charge my laptop?

Models with 65 W+ USB-C PD charge most USB-C laptops including MacBook Air and Pro.
